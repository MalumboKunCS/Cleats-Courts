    Cleats & Courts

    Warm greetings from SparkNext Learning Hub! ✨ Cleats & Courts is a dynamic sports showcase designed to foster team bonding, collaboration, networking, and a healthy competitive spirit.

    Organized by our student executive interns – fondly known as Sparks – this event features competitive sports for all participants.

    Featured Sports

    ⚽ Football (Boys)
    Team-based competitive football — bring your energy, strategy, and sportsmanship.
    🏐 Netball (Girls)
    Fast-paced netball showcasing teamwork, coordination, and competitive spirit.

    Football will be conducted for boys and Netball for girls. This structure helps maintain balanced gameplay and allows everyone to fully engage and have fun. We truly appreciate your understanding and enthusiasm!

    What to bring

    • A bottle of water
    • Appropriate sports attire
    • An energized mindset, ready to collaborate and compete 🔥
